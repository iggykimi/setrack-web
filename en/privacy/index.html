Privacy policy

Last updated: 5 October 2026.

SetRack does not collect, transmit or share any personal data. The app has no internet permission: what you log (exercises, sets, weights) is stored only in your device’s local storage.

You control your data:

  • “Save backup” writes a file to the Downloads folder when you ask for it.
  • Android backup (if it is enabled on your system) may include the app’s data; it is managed from your device settings.
  • Uninstalling the app deletes all data.

There are no analytics, ads, trackers or third-party services. The app does not declare the internet permission; its only system permission is vibration, used for the tick when you log a set.

Contact: open an issue on GitHub.
