Privacy policy · full text ▾

SetRack collects no data. It works fully offline and declares no permissions at all: no internet, no location, no camera, no contacts, no storage. Everything you log stays on your device; uninstalling removes everything. No analytics, no ads, no trackers, no third-party services.

La app no tiene permiso de internet: la información que registras (ejercicios, series, pesos) se guarda únicamente en el almacenamiento local de tu dispositivo. Before you install

Do I need an account?▾

No. You open the app and train. No sign-up, no email, no passwords.

Does it work offline?▾

Yes. It is 100% offline: the app does not even have internet permission. It works the same in a basement with no signal.

Where is my data?▾

On your phone and only on your phone. No cloud, no analytics, no trackers. You make the backup: CSV export or a file in Downloads.

Can I add my own exercises?▾

Yes. The catalog with muscle and equipment is the starting point: create yours with their muscle and equipment.

How much does it cost?▾

Nothing. Free, no ads, no in-app purchases.

When will it be on Google Play?▾

It is in internal testing. When the public version ships, the link will be here.
