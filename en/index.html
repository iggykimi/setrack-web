Privacy policy · full text +

SetRack collects no data. It works fully offline and declares no internet permission; the only system permission is vibration, used for the tick when logging a set. No analytics, no ads, no trackers, no third-party services.

Texto original en español (versión registrada): SetRack no recoge, transmite ni comparte ningún dato personal. La app no tiene permiso de internet: la información que registras (ejercicios, series, pesos) se guarda únicamente en el almacenamiento local de tu dispositivo. No hay analítica, anuncios, rastreadores ni servicios de terceros. La app no declara el permiso de internet; el único permiso del sistema es la vibración, que usa para el toque al apuntar cada serie. Before you install

Do I need an account?+

No. You open the app and train. No sign-up, no email, no passwords.

Does it work offline?+

Yes. It is 100% offline: the app does not even have internet permission. It works the same in a basement with no signal.

Where is my data?+

On your phone and only on your phone. No cloud, no analytics, no trackers. You make the backup: CSV export or a file in Downloads.

Can I add my own exercises?+

Yes. The catalog with muscle and equipment is the starting point: create yours with their muscle and equipment.

How much does it cost?+

Nothing. Free, no ads, no in-app purchases.

When will it be on Google Play?+

It is in internal testing. When the public version ships, the link will be here.
