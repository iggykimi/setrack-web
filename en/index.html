Questionspeople ask.

What is SetRack?
SetRack is a free gym log for Android that works with no internet: it has no ads, needs no account and keeps your sets only on your phone. It includes 104 exercises, 3 starter programs, estimated 1RM with the Brzycki formula and backup to Downloads with CSV export. The app is in 11 languages; this site is in 6.
Does it work offline?
Yes. The app declares no internet permission, so it logs the same in a basement with no signal. Its only permission is vibration, for the tick when you log a set.
How much does it cost?
Nothing. It is free, with no ads. If you find it useful, you can buy me a coffee on Ko-fi.
What if I change phones?
Save a backup to Downloads and restore it on the new phone, or export your sets to CSV. Uninstalling deletes the data, so back up first.
Do I need an account?
No. No sign-up and no email. Your data stays on your phone.

Privacy policy

Last updated: 5 October 2026.

SetRack does not collect, transmit or share any personal data. The app has no internet permission: what you log (exercises, sets, weights) is stored only in your device’s local storage.

You control your data:

  • “Save backup” writes a file to the Downloads folder when you ask for it.
  • Android backup (if it is enabled on your system) may include the app’s data; it is managed from your device settings.
  • Uninstalling the app deletes all data.

There are no analytics, ads, trackers or third-party services. The app does not declare the internet permission; its only system permission is vibration, used for the tick when you log a set.
